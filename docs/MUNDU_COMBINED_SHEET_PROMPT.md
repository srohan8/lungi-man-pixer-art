# Mundu finale: one combined sprite sheet (Kannan and Pey Komban in the same frames)

Why: the scene mixes separate Kannan and Pey Komban sprites that were drawn at different scales, so the swing never touches the tusk and the proportions drift. One sheet where both are drawn together fixes the contact and the scale.

## Proportions (the important part)
- Pey Komban standing upright = 100% of the cell height (about 2.3 m tall, a huge tusked demon).
- Kannan standing upright = 48% of Pey Komban's height (about 1.7 m man next to a giant). In the game they are 28 px and 60 px.
- Kannan's head reaches roughly Pey Komban's belt. The tusk tips are at about 70% of Pey Komban's height.
- When Kannan stands on Pey Komban's head he is about 48% tall again, with his feet on top of the head.
- The same size for both characters in every frame, only the pose changes. Same ground line in every cell (feet on one baseline, dust and shadows below it).
- Kannan always on the LEFT of the cell, Pey Komban on the RIGHT, facing each other, so the game can mirror the whole frame when needed.

## Sheet: 10 frames in one row (cell about 3:2 wide), plain white background, both characters in every cell
Kannan: curly dark hair, aviator sunglasses, moustache, green coconut satchel, bare chest; white mundu with a gold border (he loses it in frame 1 and wears black shorts after).
Pey Komban: huge dark-skinned tusked demon, bronze arm bands, ragged loincloth, red glowing eyes.
1. Standoff: Kannan in a wide stance whipping the mundu off his waist, Pey Komban dazed, hand to his face.
2. Kannan whirls the mundu overhead as a lasso (shouting), Pey Komban stunned and swaying.
3. Kannan throws: arm fully extended, the mundu flying across the cell and looping round Pey Komban's tusk.
4. The mundu is knotted round the tusks and pulled taut between them, Kannan leaning back with both hands on the cloth, Pey Komban's head yanked forward and down.
5. Kannan leaps up the taut cloth, feet leaving the ground, Pey Komban bending as the cloth drags him.
6. Kannan at the top of the swing above Pey Komban's head, the cloth taut to the tusks, his legs tucked.
7. Kannan lands crouched on Pey Komban's head, both hands pulling the knotted mundu, his mouth open in an angry roar, Pey Komban bellowing.
8. The hit: Pey Komban's head wrenched back, Kannan flung backwards off him, arms out, the mundu streaming.
9. Pey Komban toppling backwards, arms flung up; Kannan landing in a crouch on the left.
10. Pey Komban lying flat on his back in a cloud of dust, the mundu across his chest; Kannan standing, chest heaving, a faint golden glow.

Style: same art style, line weight and colours as my other hero and Pey Komban sheets, clean dark outline, flat cel shading, no white sticker outline, no scenery, no text. Each frame fully inside its cell with margin, characters never cropped.
