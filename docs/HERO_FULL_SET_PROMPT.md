## Rules for every sheet
Plain flat white background or real transparency; no checkerboard, no ground, no shadow, no glow, no white outline, no text. Side view facing right. Healthy and clean: curly dark hair, aviator sunglasses, moustache, bare chest, green coconut satchel across the chest, white mundu with a gold border. Same height in every frame (a normal adult), feet on one baseline, every frame fully inside its cell.

## Sheet 1: idle and run (10 frames, one row)
1-2. Idle: relaxed and ready, knees slightly bent, chest rising, a little weight shift (loops).
3-10. Run cycle, 8 frames, a fast confident run, torso leaning forward, arms pumping, the mundu and satchel swinging; one full loop (contact, down, passing, up, then the other leg).

## Sheet 2: jump (6 frames, one row)
1. Crouch: knees bent, arms swinging back.
2. Take-off: pushing off, arms swinging up, one foot leaving the ground.
3. Rising: both feet off the ground, knees tucked, arms up.
4. Peak: stretched out, hanging for a moment, mundu flaring.
5. Falling: legs reaching down, arms up and out.
6. Landing: knees absorbing the thud, arms down, a small dust puff at the feet (no shadow).

## Sheet 3: rope swing and throw (7 frames, one row)
1. Grabbing the rope: one arm stretched up gripping it.
2. Swinging back: both hands on the rope, body hanging, legs behind.
3. Swinging forward: legs thrust ahead, body stretched.
4. Letting go: rope hand opening, body launched forward.
5. Throw, wind-up: arm drawn back with a coconut in the hand, weight on the back foot.
6. Throw, release: arm extended forward, coconut leaving the hand, weight forward.
7. Throw while running: the same release pose with the legs mid-stride.
The rope is not drawn (the game draws it).
