# Mundu finale: art prompts (Pey Komban knockout scene)

Why: the scene is drawn in code with tiny sprites, and the key moment (the mundu tied around Pey Komban) is not shown at all.
Plan: the art below becomes sprite sheets that the game animates (same way as the trishul sheet), so the scene plays as a real animation with the chenda track and Kannan's scream.

Attach as references to every prompt: `peykomban_sheet.png` (Pey Komban design) and `Kannan Trident Action Sprite Sheet.png` (Kannan design).
Common style line (put it in every prompt): same art style, line weight, colours and proportions as the references, clean dark outline, flat cel shading, plain white background, every frame fully inside its cell with margin, no ground, no scenery, no text.

## Sheet A: Kannan with the mundu (8 frames, one row, facing right)
Kannan: curly dark hair, aviator sunglasses, moustache, green coconut satchel, bare chest, white mundu with a gold border.
1. Whipping the mundu off his waist with one hand, the cloth unwinding, determined face.
2. Whirling the mundu overhead like a lasso, the cloth stretched in a loop, mouth open in a shout.
3. Whirling the mundu overhead, the other half of the turn, the loop on the other side of his head.
4. Throwing the loop forward, arm fully extended, the cloth flying out in a long line.
5. Swinging through the air on a rope, one hand on the rope, legs tucked, mundu cloth trailing.
6. Leaping down head-first towards a target, arms forward, hair and satchel streaming upward.
7. Crouched on top of a huge head, both hands gripping the taut mundu, leaning back, pulling hard, face twisted in an angry roar with the mouth wide open.
8. Standing victorious, the mundu back in one hand, chest heaving, a faint golden glow around him.

## Sheet B: Pey Komban reacting (6 frames, one row, facing left, same size as the reference)
Pey Komban: huge dark-skinned tusked demon, bronze arm bands, ragged loincloth, red glowing eyes.
1. Staggering backwards, dazed, one hand to his face, tusks lowered.
2. Stunned, head hanging, eyes squeezed shut, wobbling on his feet.
3. THE KEY FRAME: a white mundu is knotted tight around his tusks and neck, his head yanked backwards and sideways by the cloth, mouth open in a bellow, hands clawing at the cloth.
4. Toppling over backwards, arms flung out, the mundu still around his tusks.
5. Lying flat on his back on the ground, dust around him, the mundu loose across his chest.
6. Dissolving into golden light: the same lying pose made of glowing gold, partly turning into sparkles and rising light.
