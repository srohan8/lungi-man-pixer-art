# Privacy policy — Kanjiravanam Chronicles

Draft for the current build only. Do not publish until the final build is reviewed. This draft does not cover the planned advertising and purchases.

Developer: Moonga Studio

## Information stored by the game

Kanjiravanam Chronicles stores your high score, progress, whether you have seen the prologue, and sound preference locally on your device. The current game code does not send these values to Moonga Studio or run an analytics service.

The current build has no player accounts, advertisements, or in-app purchases. Game artwork and audio are bundled with the app. The game does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

## Removing saved information

You can clear the game's saved information through Android's app storage settings. This resets progress, high scores, and preferences. Android backup and restore may retain or restore app information according to your device and Google account settings; backup is currently enabled in the Android manifest.

## Google Play

Google Play distributes the app and may process information independently under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Changes

This policy will be updated when the game's data practices change. Planned ads and purchases are not included in the current build. Before those features ship, this policy must describe the actual providers, data practices, consent choices, and purchase handling.
