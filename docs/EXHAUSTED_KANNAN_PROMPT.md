Attach as references: `Kannan Trident Action Sprite Sheet.png` (design, size, line weight) and `Kerala Disco Dance Hero Sprite Sheet.png` (same style, newest).

## Art prompt: one sheet, one row of 8 frames, side view facing right
Plain flat white background (or real transparency). No checkerboard, ground, shadow, glow, white outline or text. Same character, art style, line weight, colours and proportions as the references: curly dark hair (messy now), aviator sunglasses (one lens cracked, slightly crooked), moustache, bare chest, green coconut satchel (strap torn, hanging low), white mundu with a gold border (dirty, torn at the hem). Every frame fully inside its cell with margin, the same size in every frame, feet or knees on the same baseline.
He is BEATEN UP and EXHAUSTED: scratches and purple bruises on the chest, arms and face, a cut above the eyebrow, mud and soot smudges, sweat running down, shaking, shoulders heaving.
1. Standing hunched, hands on his knees, head down, chest heaving in (inhale), mouth open.
2. Same pose, chest heaving out (exhale), shoulders dropped lower, a little puff of breath.
3. Sinking: one knee giving way, one hand pressed to his side, wincing, eyes squeezed shut.
4. Kneeling on both knees, both hands flat on the ground, head hanging, sweat drops falling.
5. Kneeling, head lifted, looking up with a drained, despairing face, mouth open as if saying "I cannot do this any more".
6. Kneeling, head hanging again, one hand on his chest, panting (the same as frame 4 but a different breath, so frames 4 and 6 loop as panting).
7. Falling, front view or three-quarter view: arms flung up, hair and mundu streaming upward, mouth wide open in a scream, eyes wide.
8. Floating, drifting down gently: body relaxed and slightly curled, arms loose and out to the sides, mundu and hair floating, eyes closed or half closed, a faint golden glow of light is allowed only as small sparkles, not around the body.
